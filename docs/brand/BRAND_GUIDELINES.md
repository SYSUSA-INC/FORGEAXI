# ForgeAXI Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Brand Standard  
**Primary identity:** Ice / Midnight

---

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This file is the authoritative working brand specification for ForgeAXI. Use it when creating product UI, websites, presentations, proposal materials, marketing assets, social media, documentation, application icons, diagrams, and future brand collateral.

The intent is to preserve a consistent, premium enterprise identity while allowing the ForgeAXI product and brand system to scale.

### Non-Negotiable Principle

Do not redesign, reinterpret, regenerate, or casually modify the approved ForgeAXI logo. Derivative assets must originate from the approved master artwork once production vector files are established.

# 2. Brand Foundation

## 2.1 What ForgeAXI Is

ForgeAXI is a multi-tenant proposal lifecycle platform that unifies:

- Opportunity capture
- Capture and pursuit management
- Proposal management
- Collaborative response development
- Stakeholder communication
- Review and approval
- Submission management
- Proposal knowledge and reuse

ForgeAXI is broader than a document-authoring tool or proposal content repository. The brand should represent the complete pursuit lifecycle.

## 2.2 Brand Purpose

ForgeAXI exists to bring structure, intelligence, collaboration, visibility, and control to the complete proposal lifecycle.

The platform connects activities that are frequently fragmented across spreadsheets, documents, email, chat, task-management systems, repositories, and isolated proposal tools.

## 2.3 Positioning

> **ForgeAXI is the connected proposal lifecycle platform that transforms fragmented pursuit activities into a coordinated path from opportunity to submission.**

## 2.4 Brand Promise

> **One connected pursuit. From opportunity to submission.**

ForgeAXI gives organizations a structured environment to capture opportunities, coordinate teams, develop responses, manage communication, maintain visibility, and advance submissions with greater control.

## 2.5 Core Brand Idea

**Convergence → Coordination → Momentum**

The ForgeAXI X embodies this concept.

Independent workstreams intersect:

- Opportunities
- Requirements
- People
- Content
- Communications
- Reviews
- Decisions
- Approvals
- Submissions

ForgeAXI brings those workstreams together and moves the pursuit forward.

## 2.6 Brand Attributes

ForgeAXI should consistently feel:

### Intelligent
Sophisticated without appearing complicated.

### Decisive
Focused on action, progress, readiness, and outcomes.

### Connected
Reflecting collaboration across people, information, processes, and organizations.

### Structured
Bringing discipline and visibility to complex proposal activity.

### Modern
Contemporary enterprise technology without chasing short-lived design trends.

### Confident
Clear and authoritative without exaggerated claims or unnecessary hype.

## 2.7 Brand Principles

### CONNECT
Bring opportunities, teams, information, communication, and proposal activity together.

### ORCHESTRATE
Coordinate the moving pieces of a pursuit through a structured lifecycle.

### RESPOND
Enable teams to create stronger, more coordinated proposal responses.

### ADVANCE
Keep pursuits progressing toward review, approval, submission, and outcome.

### LEARN
Turn completed pursuits into reusable organizational knowledge.

# 4. Logo System

## 4.1 Primary Logo Configuration

The official primary ForgeAXI logo uses a **horizontal lockup**:

**[ForgeAXI X Emblem] + [ForgeAXI Wordmark]**

The emblem appears on the **left** and the ForgeAXI wordmark appears on the **right**.

This configuration should be treated as the default logo unless space or application constraints require the standalone emblem.

## 4.2 Approved Primary Logo Colors

### Emblem
- Field: ForgeAXI Ice `#45CBE8`
- X within emblem: White `#FFFFFF`

### Wordmark
- Primary lettering: ForgeAXI Midnight `#101D35`
- Signature X accent: ForgeAXI Ice `#45CBE8`

### Background
- Master artwork: Transparent
- Preferred placement: White or very light neutral surface

## 4.3 Signature X

The X is the principal recognizable visual device of the ForgeAXI identity.

Conceptually it represents:

- Convergence of workstreams
- Coordination
- Intersection of people and information
- Connected workflows
- Forward movement
- Transformation of distributed activity into a unified pursuit

The geometry of the approved X must not be redrawn or substituted.

## 4.4 Emblem

The standalone emblem consists of the approved X inside the Ice field.

Approved uses include:

- SaaS application icon
- Favicon
- Browser tab
- Mobile application icon
- Product navigation
- Social avatar
- Loading state or splash screen
- Compact UI environments
- Branded avatar where full wordmark is impractical

The emblem should not replace the full horizontal logo when establishing the ForgeAXI brand for the first time in a communication.

## 4.5 Lockup Relationship

The relationship between emblem and wordmark is fixed in the master artwork.

Target construction for production vector refinement:

- Emblem positioned left
- Emblem optically vertically centered with wordmark
- Emblem visual height approximately `1.10–1.20×` the wordmark capital height
- Horizontal gap approximately `0.30–0.35×` the emblem width

Once the production master is established, preserve the exact ratio rather than manually recreating these measurements.

## 4.6 Clear Space

Define **X-space** as approximately 25% of the emblem width.

Minimum clear space around the complete horizontal logo:

- **Standard:** `1X`
- **Premium / hero placement:** `2X` or more

No text, imagery, rule, border, UI control, icon, or other visual element should intrude into the clear-space zone.

## 4.7 Minimum Size

### Digital

Horizontal logo:
- Absolute recommended minimum width: `160 px`
- Preferred normal usage: `200 px` or larger

Standalone emblem:
- Absolute recommended minimum: `24 × 24 px`
- Preferred UI size: `32 × 32 px` or larger

Favicons should use separately optimized versions at:

- `16 × 16 px`
- `32 × 32 px`
- `48 × 48 px`

Do not create favicons by blindly shrinking a large raster image.

### Print

Horizontal logo:
- Recommended minimum width: `1.5 in / 38 mm`

Standalone emblem:
- Recommended minimum width: `0.25 in / 6.35 mm`

## 4.8 Light-Surface Logo

Preferred version:

- Midnight wordmark
- Ice X accent
- Ice emblem
- White emblem X
- Transparent background

Use on white and approved very-light neutral surfaces.

## 4.9 Dark-Surface / Reversed Logo

For Midnight or comparably dark surfaces, create a controlled reversed master version using:

- White primary lettering
- Ice X accent
- Ice emblem
- White emblem X

Do not place Midnight lettering on a dark background where contrast is insufficient.

## 4.10 Monochrome Versions

Maintain two approved monochrome master files:

### Midnight Monochrome
Use on white or light surfaces when color reproduction is unavailable.

### White Monochrome
Use on sufficiently dark surfaces.

The signature X geometry must remain intact even when color differentiation is removed.

## 4.11 Logo Prohibitions

Never:

- Redraw or alter the X geometry
- Replace Ice with an arbitrary accent color
- Stretch, compress, skew, or rotate the logo
- Change the horizontal emblem-left configuration of the primary master
- Move or resize emblem and wordmark independently
- Re-type the ForgeAXI wordmark using an ordinary font
- Change the spacing between emblem and wordmark
- Add shadows
- Add bevels
- Add glow
- Add metallic effects
- Apply gradients to the production master
- Add outlines
- Apply textures to the logo
- Place the standard logo over visually noisy imagery
- Add an enclosing shape around the full horizontal lockup
- Randomly recolor individual letters
- Separate `Forge` and `AXI` into unrelated type treatments
- Use the logo as a repeating decorative pattern
- Place taglines inside the protected logo lockup

# 7. Functional Product Colors

Functional colors communicate state and should not become competing brand colors.

| State | HEX | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Success | `#16865C` | Positive / completed / approved |
| Warning | `#D98B16` | Attention / risk / pending |
| Critical | `#C83E4D` | Error / overdue / blocking issue |
| Information | `#45CBE8` | Informational / connected / active where accessible |

For product implementation, validate text/background pairings against WCAG contrast requirements before release. Do not assume that a brand color automatically provides sufficient text contrast at every size.

---

# 8. Color Balance

Recommended starting balance for corporate and marketing materials:

- `55–65%` White / very light neutral
- `25–35%` Midnight / dark neutrals
- `5–10%` Ice
- `0–5%` functional/status colors

This is a compositional guideline, not a mandatory mathematical formula.

The key principle is restraint: **Ice should remain a recognizable signature rather than becoming visual wallpaper.**

# 9. Typography

## 9.1 Official Logo Typography

The ForgeAXI wordmark is custom artwork.

**Never recreate the official logo by typing `ForgeAXI` in the brand's supporting typeface.**

Use approved vector or high-resolution master logo assets.

## 9.2 Primary Brand and Product Typeface

### Inter

Use **Inter** as the primary ForgeAXI digital, product, web, and brand-supporting type family.

Why Inter fits the identity:

- Excellent screen legibility
- Strong performance at small UI sizes
- Broad weight range
- Clear numerals for dashboards and proposal metrics
- Modern enterprise character
- Neutral enough to support rather than compete with the custom wordmark

Recommended weights:

- Regular `400`
- Medium `500`
- SemiBold `600`
- Bold `700`

Avoid unnecessary use of very light weights in operational interfaces.

## 9.3 Microsoft 365 Fallback

### Aptos

Use Aptos where Inter is unavailable, especially in:

- Microsoft Word
- PowerPoint
- Excel
- Outlook
- Microsoft 365 templates

## 9.4 Digital Font Stack

```css
font-family: Inter, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;
```

## 9.5 Microsoft-Centric Font Stack

```css
font-family: Aptos, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;
```

# 11. Typographic Behavior

## 11.1 Headings

Prefer sentence case.

Preferred:

> Manage every pursuit from opportunity to submission

Avoid routine all-caps headlines:

> MANAGE EVERY PURSUIT FROM OPPORTUNITY TO SUBMISSION

Uppercase is appropriate for short labels, metadata, compact navigation cues, and carefully controlled graphic treatments.

## 11.2 Alignment

Default alignment: **Left**

Center alignment may be used selectively for:

- Marketing hero statements
- Event signage
- Short brand statements
- Focused callouts

Operational product interfaces should remain predominantly left aligned.

## 11.3 Weight

Use weight to create hierarchy, not decoration.

Avoid entire body paragraphs in SemiBold or Bold.

## 11.4 Tracking

Starting guidance:

- Large display/headlines: `-1%` to `-2%`
- Body: normal/default
- Short uppercase labels: `+4%` to `+8%`

Always prioritize legibility over mechanical adherence to tracking values.

# 13. Multi-Tenant and Co-Branding Principles

ForgeAXI is a multi-tenant platform. Customer identity must therefore be supported without weakening the ForgeAXI master brand.

## 13.1 Product Shell

ForgeAXI should remain identifiable as the platform provider through the primary product shell, login experience, or controlled `Powered by ForgeAXI` treatment.

Tenant branding may appear in designated customer-brand areas without altering the ForgeAXI logo itself.

## 13.2 Customer Logos

Never:

- Merge a customer logo into the ForgeAXI logo
- Recolor ForgeAXI to match a tenant's palette
- Alter the ForgeAXI emblem to incorporate customer initials
- Create tenant-specific versions of the master ForgeAXI X

## 13.3 SYSUSA Relationship

Where SYSUSA and ForgeAXI appear together, treat them as separate identities with sufficient clear space. Do not fuse their marks or create a combined emblem unless a separate co-brand standard is formally approved.

# 14. Accessibility and Digital Implementation

Accessibility is part of the brand standard, not an optional later-stage treatment.

Product and web teams should:

- Validate released text/background combinations against applicable WCAG contrast requirements
- Use color plus another indicator for status wherever practical
- Avoid using Ice as small body text on white without validating contrast
- Preserve visible focus states
- Maintain legible type sizes and line heights
- Provide semantic labels for logo imagery where required
- Use SVG master assets for scalable digital use whenever possible

# 15. Asset Mastering Requirements

Before the identity is treated as production-final, establish exact master logo artwork rather than repeatedly regenerating the logo through image generation.

Required master assets:

1. Horizontal primary logo: transparent SVG
2. Horizontal primary logo: transparent PNG
3. Reversed horizontal logo: SVG and PNG
4. Midnight monochrome logo: SVG and PNG
5. White monochrome logo: SVG and PNG
6. Standalone emblem: SVG and PNG
7. App icon master
8. Favicon set
9. Print-ready vector master: PDF/EPS or equivalent production format

All derivative assets must originate from the same approved vector geometry.

# 17. Instructions for AI Systems and Creative Tools

When this Markdown file is provided to Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, a designer, developer, or another content-generation system:

1. Treat the **Locked Brand Decisions** section as authoritative.
2. Do not invent alternative ForgeAXI logo designs unless explicitly asked.
3. Do not substitute different blues for Midnight or Ice in the master logo.
4. Do not recreate the logo from text alone when approved artwork is available.
5. Preserve the capitalization exactly as **ForgeAXI**.
6. Keep brand writing concise, credible, enterprise-oriented, and human.
7. Avoid generic AI and SaaS marketing clichés.
8. Position ForgeAXI around the complete proposal/pursuit lifecycle, not merely proposal writing.
9. Use the X concept selectively as a visual metaphor for convergence, coordination, and forward movement.
10. Keep Ice relatively scarce so it remains a signature brand cue.
11. Use functional colors to communicate state, not to expand the core brand palette.
12. Do not modify the primary logo because a particular layout is inconvenient; use an approved secondary asset instead.
13. When producing customer-facing product designs, support tenant identity without allowing tenant branding to alter the ForgeAXI master identity.
14. When information in a future prompt conflicts with this file, do not silently modify the brand standard. Flag the conflict unless the prompt explicitly authorizes a brand change.
